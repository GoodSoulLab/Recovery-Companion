Privacy, evidence, and review status

Local saving: Saved resource IDs are stored in this browser on this device using local storage. Search terms, choices, and personal notes are not saved. No account, analytics, or app server receives saved items. Anyone who can use this browser profile may be able to see them. Use “Clear saved” from the Saved view to remove them.

Limits: Evidence badges describe research on a full approach or related population; they do not establish that a short card is effective. Direct evidence about treatments for CSBD/PPU remains limited. ACT/CBT/DBT/IFS are not interchangeable. Peer fellowship materials and sponsor guidance are recovery resources, not clinical evidence. A polished interface does not mean these drafts are clinically approved.

This prototype is not monitored or crisis support. If you need immediate help, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional. Do not use this page to process trauma memories. All contact links require your own action.
