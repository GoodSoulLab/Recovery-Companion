# Recovery Companion — Product Lab

Research and prototype workspace for evaluating whether a privacy-conscious, clinically governed, user-directed recovery companion should exist.

**Status:** Product discovery. No clinical validation, participant findings, or commercial validation. Draft exercises are for qualified review only.

## Current decision

Test a narrow hypothesis before making a venture or product commitment: does a transparent selector, using only a user-selected situation and preferences, help people choose an acceptable next action faster or with better fit than the same options in a well-organized static toolkit?

The current public-source evidence shows that integration alone is not a defensible novelty claim. Relay publicly describes plans, therapist-designed lessons, check-ins, urge/trigger logging, and peer support; SMART Recovery offers free tools and meetings. These are publisher claims, not authenticated app tests, independent audits, or clinical outcome evidence.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Adults who voluntarily seek behavior change; no diagnosis required.
- Do not pathologize high libido, pornography use, moral incongruence, or a screening score.
- No passive sexual/browsing surveillance, unrestricted AI therapy, automatic disclosure, compulsory abstinence, or trauma processing.
- Keep peer support voluntary and independent; do not present the prototype as treatment, monitoring, or crisis response.
- Exercise drafts remain unreviewed until qualified clinical and lived-experience review is recorded.
- No participant research until safety, privacy, intellectual-property, accessibility, and ethics gates are met.
- Preserve null results; stop or pivot if existing alternatives are sufficient.

## Current gate

A public-site walkthrough of Relay and SMART is documented; the authenticated Relay, Fortify, and Brainbuddy app experiences remain untested. Synthetic testing exposed v0.1 selector defects. Prototype v0.2 corrects the preselected filters, first-three cap, and Browse all behavior; offline logic checks confirmed 12/12 cards for neutral and Browse all, both human-support cards on the support route, and a clear fallback for incompatible filters. Static checks found no external dependencies, network calls, or persistent browser storage. These are mechanics checks, not accessibility, clinical, privacy-security, or user validation. The prototype still contains unreviewed draft exercises, so it remains expert-review only. No participant exposure or sensitive research until qualified clinical and lived-experience review, privacy/security, IP, accessibility, and ethics gates are met.
