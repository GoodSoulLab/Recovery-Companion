# Competitive Evidence Matrix v0.1
Date: 2026-10-11
Method: Public vendor sites and app-store disclosures; NOT authenticated hands-on teardown or independent security audit. 'Unverified' is not 'absent'.

| Product | Verified public-facing proposition | Relevant challenge to our thesis | Unverified / follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relay Health | Small peer groups, daily check-ins, urge/trigger logs, therapist-designed lessons, personalized plans; $149/year on official site; therapist partnership and referral workflow | Already integrates skills, accountability, in-the-moment human connection, and between-session support | Direct experience quality; clinical outcomes; cross-philosophy flexibility; actual data flows; group safety; therapist integration implementation |
| Fortify – Find Freedom | App Store privacy disclosures include health, user content, and sensitive information potentially linked to identity; marketed recovery app | Existing category incumbent | Current feature-level experience, actual intervention-selection rules, security architecture, independently measured outcomes |
| Brainbuddy | Recovery tracking/check-ins; privacy policy says no data sale, no ads, account deletion, limited analytics including advertising measurement | Privacy claims and recovery content already exist | Direct experience, exercise provenance, privacy verification, clinical outcomes |
| SMART Recovery | Free online/in-person peer meetings, voluntary participation, practical tools, mobile meeting finder | Free secular skills and connection are available without building a new community | Suitability for specific sexual-behavior concerns, outcomes, real-time access to support |
| Proposed Recovery Companion | Transparent selection among reviewed tools, user-chosen practices, and voluntary connection, with data-minimal defaults | Hypothesized cross-approach fit, autonomy, and privacy advantage | ALL product advantages remain untested |

## Hands-on audit script (not yet executed)
For each product: 1) start without revealing unnecessary sensitive details; 2) locate help for urge, overwhelm, setback, connection, and values-based action; 3) record time, clicks, suitability, freedom to reject, price/paywall, and data requests; 4) review privacy settings, exports, deletion, notification defaults, and sharing; 5) distinguish actual experience from marketing; 6) document screenshots only where lawful and privacy-safe.

## Decision-critical unknowns
- Does any competitor already provide reviewed, explainable cross-modality selection?
- Can users find an appropriate tool faster than with a static toolkit?
- Do people prefer human connection to AI support?
- Is privacy-first differentiation real and economically sustainable?
