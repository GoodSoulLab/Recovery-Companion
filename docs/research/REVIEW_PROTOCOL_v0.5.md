# Recovery Companion — Reviewer and Formative Test Protocol v0.5

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Status:** Draft; not approved for participant use  
**Prototype:** [Resource Bank v0.7](../../prototype/resource-bank-v0.7.html)  
**Companion content brief:** [Resource Bank evidence brief v0.1](RESOURCE_BANK_EVIDENCE_BRIEF_v0.1.md)

## Decision question

Does a small, evidence-labeled resource bank help adults voluntarily seeking behavior change identify an acceptable next step and understand support roles better than an unstructured resource list or their current path to care/recovery resources?

The experiment is about access, fit, comprehension, autonomy, and privacy understanding. It cannot establish clinical efficacy, behavior change, treatment equivalence, or sponsor quality.

## Current hard gate

Do not expose potential participants to this exact exercise content until all items below are resolved and documented for the frozen build.

| Gate | Status | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical content | Open | Qualified practicing clinician review of each exact resource, evidence badge, limit, and support boundary; verify current licensure, relevant scope, and conflicts. |
| Lived experience | Open | Paid review by 2–4 adults with varied self-chosen goals; no detailed disclosure required; revise for agency, shame, clarity, accessibility, and felt safety. |
| IFS-inspired card | Open / heightened | Reviewer with relevant IFS clinical training plus trauma-informed lived-experience review; remove card unless the narrow non-memory wording is acceptable. |
| Fellowship/IP | Open | Confirm current SAA links and exact scope of claims; keep their copyrighted literature linked, not reproduced. Confirm original authorship/licensing of every prompt and graphic. |
| Privacy/security | Open | Independent inspection of delivery and browser behavior; confirm storage key/content, clear behavior, shared-device disclosure, network requests, and host connection metadata. |
| Accessibility | Open | Rendered browser, keyboard-only, 200–400% zoom/reflow, screen-reader, reduced motion, focus and contrast review. |
| Ethics | Open | Determine whether IRB or other oversight applies; document determination, consent, compensation, adverse-event plan, data retention/deletion, and referral boundaries. |

Any material safety disagreement blocks the relevant resource. A green check or successful source test does not satisfy these gates.

## Smallest formative test after gates close

- Eight paid adults (18+) who voluntarily express interest in behavior change; no diagnosis, score, abstinence commitment, or detailed sexual history required.
- Use non-graphic, fictional situations. Participants may skip any task or stop without losing compensation.
- Ask each person to locate an acceptable next step in the bank and in one comparison path: an unstructured static list or their chosen current route. Counterbalance order.
- Do not request use of an exercise in real life, disclosure to a sponsor/partner, an abstinence commitment, or private browsing/device data.
- Observe time to find, task completion, confidence in fit, whether the person understands evidence badges, ability to reject/stop, shame/distress signals, and whether the participant understands that saved IDs are local and potentially visible to other users of that browser profile.
- Ask whether the bank adds value compared with direct SAA, SMART, CSAT/clinician, or self-chosen resources. Do not infer unmet need from negative feedback about an existing support system.
- Do not store sexual histories, search terms, or prototype event analytics. Research notes should be minimal, consented, access-controlled, and scheduled for deletion.

## Precommitted decision rules

**Stop/pivot** if a participant interprets the bank as diagnosis, clinical approval, a demand for abstinence, surveillance, or crisis support; if a card prompts notable distress or trauma processing; or if local saving is misunderstood in a way that puts privacy at risk.

**Revise and retest** if several people cannot distinguish treatment evidence from card-level evidence, misunderstand peer sponsor limits, cannot clear saved items, or cannot find a useful option without extra explanation.

**Continue to a larger comparison only if** most participants can independently find and accurately describe a suitable option, preserve a sense of choice, understand the storage disclosure, and find the bank at least as useful as the direct-resource comparator. This is a design progression rule only, not a clinical threshold.

If an unstructured list or direct links perform as well, prefer that smaller solution. If the central need is an available, accountable human relationship rather than content navigation, reconsider the product scope instead of adding more exercises.

## Operational safety

- Prototype is not care, not monitored, and does not respond to crisis.
- Qualified study staff need a plan for distress, voluntary stop, referral, and immediate safety concerns before research begins.
- No automated outreach, sponsor matching, account, monitoring, or upload of saved items.
- Use only resources appropriate to the participant's region and verify them before study use.

## Decision record

At review completion, version the artifact and record each reviewer role, date, approve/revise/remove decision, rationale, and unresolved disagreement. Do not publish private reviewer names, contact details, personal narratives, or compensation records in this repository.
